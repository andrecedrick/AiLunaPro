What it is

The AI ROI Calculator turns four simple inputs — your team size, the monthly hours spent on repetitive work, your average hourly cost, and the workflow you want to improve — into a clear estimate of your potential savings. It is free, deterministic, and needs no account.

It estimates your:

  • Estimated monthly and yearly cost saved
  • Hours saved per month
  • Indicative payback period

How it works

  1. Enter four quick inputs about your team and a target workflow.
  2. See your estimated monthly and yearly savings, hours saved, and payback — computed instantly with conservative, rule-based assumptions.
  3. Optionally enter your email to receive the full report, with recommended AI agents for your workflow.

Benefits

  • Deterministic & conservative — rule-based estimates, not hype.

FAQ

Is the AI ROI calculator free?
Yes — it is free and requires no account. You only enter an email if you want the full report.
How is the estimate calculated?
It multiplies your monthly repetitive hours by a conservative automation savings rate for your chosen workflow, then by your average hourly cost, and annualizes it (×12). It is deterministic — no AI or LLM — so the same inputs always give the same result.
How accurate is it?
It is an indicative estimate based on the information you provide and conservative assumptions — a starting business case, not a guarantee.
What currency does it show?
Amounts are calculated in USD and displayed in your local currency as an approximate (≈) conversion.
Does it replace a full AI audit?
No — it is a quick estimate. The full AiLunaPro audit gives a complete readiness score and action plan.
