What it is

The AI Maturity Diagnostic scores eight short questions about how your organization uses and governs AI — covering governance, data readiness, security, transparency, human oversight, skills, and tool visibility — into a single AI maturity score from 0 to 100, with a maturity band and recommended next steps. It is free, deterministic, and needs no account.

You get:

  • A normalized AI maturity score (0–100)
  • Your maturity band — emerging, developing, or advanced
  • Recommended AiLunaPro agents matched to your stage

FAQ

Is the AI maturity diagnostic free?
Yes — it is free and requires no account. You only enter an email if you want the full report.
How is the maturity score calculated?
Eight weighted questions are scored and normalized to a 0–100 scale, mapped to a maturity band. It is deterministic — no AI or LLM — so the same answers always give the same score.
What is an AI maturity score?
A 0–100 indicator of how ready your organization is for AI across governance, data, security, transparency, oversight, skills, and tool visibility.
How does it relate to the EU AI Act?
It gives an indicative readiness signal for AI governance and the EU AI Act. It is informational only — not legal advice or a certification.
How long does it take?
About two minutes — eight short questions, no account required.
