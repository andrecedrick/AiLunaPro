What the EU AI Act is

The EU AI Act is a European Union regulation that introduces risk-based rules for artificial-intelligence systems placed on the EU market. It groups AI uses into tiers (for example: prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, and minimal-risk) and attaches different expectations to each. Obligations are being phased in over time.

Which obligations may apply depends on what an AI system does and how it is used. This page does not determine your obligations — it explains the landscape so you can prepare and seek qualified advice where needed.
